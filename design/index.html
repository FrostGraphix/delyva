One order, end to end

How a single order moves across the apps.

1Customer ordersBrowses, builds a cart, pays by card, transfer or wallet.
2Merchant acceptsLoud alert, one tap, picks a prep time.
3Rider is matchedOffer with earnings, navigates to the store, confirms pickup code.
4Live trackingCustomer sees the electric bike move on the map.
5Delivered & paidCode at the door, rider earns, merchant paid out weekly.
